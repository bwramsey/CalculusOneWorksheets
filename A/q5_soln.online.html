Given the acceleration function, the initial velocity and initial position of an object moving along a line, find the position function.
\(a(t)=4\cos {t}\), \(v(0)=2\), \(s(0)=6\).
\(v(t)=4\sin {t}+C\).
Letting \(t=0\), we get
\(v(0)=4\sin {0}+C\) and
\(2=C\).
Therefore, \(v(t)=4\sin {t}+2\).
So, \(s(t)=-4\cos {t}+2t+C\).
Letting \(t=0\), we get

\(s(0)=-4\cos {0}+2(0)+C\).
So,
\(6=-4+C\),
and \(C=10\).
Therefore,
\(s(t)=-4\cos {t}+2t+10\).
