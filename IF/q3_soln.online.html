Evaluate the following limits.
(a)
\( \lim _{x \to \frac {\pi }{2}} \frac {\cos {(x)}}{x} \)
Apply the Quotient Law and use continuity of the numerator and denominator.
\( \lim _{x \to \frac {\pi }{2}} \frac {\cos {(x)}}{x}=\frac {0}{\frac {\pi }{2}}=0 \)
(b)
\(\lim _{x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {25-x^2 }-4}{x-4} \)
Apply the Quotient Law and use continuity.
\( \lim _{x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {25-x^2 }-4}{x-4}= \frac {\sqrt {25-(3)^2 }-4}{3-4}= \frac {4-4}{-1}=0\)
(c)
\(\lim _{x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {25-x^2 }-4}{x-3} \)
Since \(\lim _{x \to 3} (\sqrt {25-x^2 }-4) = 0\) and \(\lim _{x \to 3} {x-3} = 0\), this limit has form \(\frac {0}{0}\).
\( \lim _{x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {25-x^2 }-4}{x-3}= \lim _{x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {25-x^2 }-4}{x-3}\frac {\sqrt {25-x^2 }+4}{\sqrt {25-x^2 }+4}=\)
\( \lim _{x \to 3} \frac {25-x^2-16}{(x-3)(\sqrt {25-x^2 }+4)}= \lim _{x \to 3} \frac {9-x^2}{(x-3)(\sqrt {25-x^2 }+4)}= \lim _{x \to 3} \frac {(3+x)(3-x)}{(x-3)(\sqrt {25-x^2 }+4)}=\\ \) \(\lim _{x \to 3} \frac {-(3+x)}{\sqrt {25-x^2 }+4}=\frac {-6}{\sqrt {25-9 }+4}=\frac {-6}{\sqrt {16 }+4}=\frac {-6}{8}=\frac {-3}{4}\)
