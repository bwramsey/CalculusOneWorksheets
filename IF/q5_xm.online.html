Evaluate each of the following limits. Possible answers include a number, \(+\infty \), \(-\infty \) and “Does Not Exist” (DNE). Make sure to state the form of the limit. Justify your answer.
(a)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x \to 3^-} \frac {x^2 - 3}{x^2 - x - 6}\)
(b)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x \to 5^+} \frac {x^2 + 6}{x^2 - 3x - 10}\)
(c)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x \to 1} \frac {4-x}{x^2 - 2x + 1}\)
(d)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x \to 2} \frac {-e^x}{(2-x)^3}\)
(e)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x \to 1} \frac {\sin {x}}{\sqrt {2-x^{2}}-1}\)
