Two polynomials, \(h\) and \(g\), are given
\[ h(x)=\frac {x^2-4}{4} \]
\[ g(x)=x-2 \]
State the form of the limit, evaluate the limit, or state that it does not exist. Justify your answer.
(a)
\(\displaystyle \lim {x \to 2}\frac {h(x)}{g(x)}\)
\(\displaystyle \lim {x \to 2}\frac {h(x)}{g(x)}=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 2}\frac {\frac {x^2-4}{4}}{x-2}\)
Form: \(\frac {{0}}{0}\)
\begin{align*} \displaystyle \lim {x \to 2}\frac {\frac {x^2-4}{4}}{x-2}&=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 2}\frac {(x-2)(x+2)}{4(x-2)}\\ &= \displaystyle \lim {x \to 2} \frac {x+2}{4}\\ &= \frac {4}{4}\\ &= 1 \end{align*}
(b)
\(\displaystyle \lim {x \to 3}\frac {g(x)-g(3)}{x-3}\)
\(\displaystyle \lim {x \to 3}\frac {g(x)-g(3)}{x-3}=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 3}\frac {x-2-1}{x-3}\)
Form: \(\frac {{0}}{0}\)
\begin{align*} \displaystyle \lim {x \to 3}\frac {g(x)-g(3)}{x-3}&=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 3}\frac {x-2-1}{x-3}\\ &=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 3}1\\ &=1 \end{align*}
(c)
\(\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\frac {h(x)-h(4)}{x-4}\)
\(\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\frac {h(x)-h(4)}{x-4}=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\frac {\frac {x^2-4}{4}-3}{x-4}\)
Form: \(\frac {{0}}{0}\)
\begin{align*} \displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\frac {h(x)-h(4)}{x-4}&=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\frac {\frac {x^2-4}{4}-3}{x-4}\\ &=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\dfrac {x^2-4-12}{4(x-4)}\\ &=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\dfrac {x^2-16}{4(x-4)}\\ &=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\dfrac {(x-4)(x+4)}{4(x-4)}\\ &=\displaystyle \lim {x \to 4}\dfrac {x+4}{4}\\ &=2 \end{align*}
