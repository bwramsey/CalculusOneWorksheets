For each of the curves given by the following equations, find a formula for the slope of the tangent line at a point \((x, y)\).
(a)
\(e^{x^2 y^3} - 5x + 7y = 36\)
\[ e^{x^2 y^3} \left ( 2xy^3 + x^2 (3y^2)\dfrac {dy}{dx} \right ) - 5 + 7\dfrac {dy}{dx} = 0 \]
\[ 2xy^3 e^{x^2y^3} + 3x^2y^2e^{x^2y^3}\dfrac {dy}{dx} - 5 + 7\dfrac {dy}{dx} = 0 \]
\[ 3x^2y^2e^{x^2y^3}\dfrac {dy}{dx} + 7\dfrac {dy}{dx} = -2xy^3 e^{x^2y^3} + 5 \]
\[ \left ( 3x^2y^2e^{x^2y^3} + 7 \right ) \dfrac {dy}{dx} = -2xy^3 e^{x^2y^3} + 5 \]
\[ \dfrac {dy}{dx} = \frac {-2xy^3 e^{x^2y^3} + 5}{3x^2 y^2 e^{x^2y^3} + 7} \]
(b)
\(7 = 22 \tan (y) + \frac {4}{x} - \frac {7}{y}\)
\[ 0 = 22 \sec ^2 (y) \dfrac {dy}{dx} - \frac {4}{x^2} + \frac {7}{y^2} \dfrac {dy}{dx} \]
\[ 22 \sec ^2(y) \dfrac {dy}{dx} + \frac {7}{y^2} \dfrac {dy}{dx} = \frac {4}{x^2} \]
\[ \dfrac {dy}{dx} = \frac {\frac {4}{x^2}}{22 \sec ^2(y) + \frac {7}{y^2}} \]
(c)
\(\cos (xy) - x^3 = 5y^3\)
\[ -\sin (xy) \cdot \left (y + x \dfrac {dy}{dx} \right ) - 3x^2 = 15y^2 \dfrac {dy}{dx} \]
\[ -y \sin (xy) - x \sin (xy) \dfrac {dy}{dx} - 3x^2 = 15y^2 \dfrac {dy}{dx} \]
\[ x \sin (xy) \dfrac {dy}{dx} + 15y^2 \dfrac {dy}{dx} = -y \sin (xy) - 3x^2 \]
\[ \dfrac {dy}{dx} = \frac {-y \sin (xy) - 3x^2}{x \sin (xy) + 15y^2} \]

Provided that \(x \sin (xy) + 15y^2 \neq 0\). It is worth pointing out that equivalent condition was not necessary for parts (a) and (b) because the denominator of those solutions cannot be \(0\).
