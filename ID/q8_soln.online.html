(a)
Let \(f\) be a positive differentiable function, defined on an open interval \(I\). Find the formula for the derivative of the function \(\ln {(f(x))}\).
\(\frac {d}{dx}\ln {(f(x))}=\dfrac {1}{f(x)}\cdot f'(x)=\dfrac {f'(x)}{f(x)}\)
(b)
Using the formula obtained in part (a), compute the derivatives of the following functions.
(i)
\(f(x)=\ln {(x^2+x+1)}\)
\(f'(x)=\dfrac {2x+1}{x^2+x+1}\)
(ii)
\(f(x)=\ln {(\sec (x) +\tan (x))}\)
\(f'(x)=\dfrac {\sec (x)\tan (x)+\sec ^2{(x)}}{\sec (x) +\tan (x)}=\dfrac {\sec (x)(\tan (x)+\sec (x))}{\sec (x) +\tan (x)}=\sec (x)\)
(iii)
\(f(x)=\ln {(\ln (x))}\)
\(f'(x)=\dfrac {\frac {1}{x}}{\ln (x)}=\dfrac {1}{x\ln (x)}\)
