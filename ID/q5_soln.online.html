The volume of a doughnut with an inner radius of \(a\) and an outer radius of \(b\) is
\[ V = \pi ^2 \frac {(b+a)(b-a)^2}{4}. \]
Find \(db/da\) if the volume of a doughnut is \(64\pi ^2\) and does not change.
We have to (implicitly) differentiate
\[ 64\pi ^2 = \pi ^2 \frac {(b+a)(b-a)^2}{4}. \]

Before doing this we’ll perform a bit of algebra to simplify our calculuations:

\begin{align*} 64\pi ^2 = \pi ^2 \frac {(b+a)(b-a)^2}{4} &\implies 256 = (b+a)(b-a)^2. \end{align*}

Now we’ll differentiate with respect to \(a\):

\begin{align*} 256 = (b+a)(b-a)^2 &\implies 0 = \left (\frac {db}{da} + 1\right )(b - a)^2 + (b + a) 2 \cdot (b - a) \cdot \left (\frac {db}{da} - 1\right ) \\ &\implies 0 = \frac {db}{da}(b-a)^2 + (b-a)^2 + 2(b^2-a^2) \frac {db}{da} - 2(b^2 - a^2). \end{align*}

To finish we solve for \(\frac {db}{da}\):

\begin{align*} 0 &= \frac {db}{da}(b-a)^2 + (b-a)^2 + 2(b^2-a^2) \frac {db}{da} - 2(b^2 - a^2) \\ &\implies - \left (\frac {db}{da}(b-a)^2 + 2(b^2-a^2) \frac {db}{da}\right ) = (b-a)^2 - 2(b^2 - a^2)\\ &\implies \frac {db}{da} = \frac {(b-a)^2 - 2(b^2 - a^2)}{-((b-a)^2 + 2(b^2-a^2))}. \end{align*}
