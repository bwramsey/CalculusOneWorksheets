• The average rate of change, \(AvRCh\), of a function \(f\) across an interval \([a,b]\) is given by

    \begin{align*} AvRCh &= \frac {\Delta f}{\Delta x} \\ &= \frac {f(b)-f(a)}{b-a} \end{align*}

  • The slope, \(m_{sec}\), of the line secant to the graph of a function \(f\) at the points \((a, f(a))\) and \((b, f(b))\) is given by

    \begin{align*} m_{sec} &= \frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} \\ &= \frac {f(b)-f(a)}{b-a} \end{align*}

  • The derivative of the function \(f\) at \(a\), denoted \(f'(a)\), is given by

    \[ f'(a) = \lim _{x \to a} \frac {f(x)-f(a)}{x-a} = \lim _{h \to 0} \frac {f(a+h)-f(a)}{h} \]
    and represents the instantaneous rate of change of \(f\) with respect to \(x\) at \(x=a\) and also the slope of the line tangent to the graph of \(y=f(x)\) at the point \((a, f(a))\).
