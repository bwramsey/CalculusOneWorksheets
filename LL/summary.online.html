0.1 The Limit Laws:

Continuity

If a function \(f\) is continuous at \(a\), then

\[ \lim _{x \to a} f(x) = f(a) \]

Composition Limit Law

If a function \(f\) is continuous at \(b= \lim _{x \to a} g(x)\), then

\[ \lim _{x \to a} f(g(x)) = f\Bigl ( \lim _{x \to a}g(x)\Bigr ) \]

For the limit laws below, assume \(\lim _{x \to a} f(x)\) and \(\lim _{x \to a} g(x)\) both exist. The following properties hold.

Sum Limit Law

\[ \lim _{x \to a} \bigl (f(x) + g(x)\bigr ) = \lim _{x \to a} f(x) + \lim _{x \to a} g(x) \]
Difference Limit Law

\[ \lim _{x \to a} \bigl (f(x) - g(x)\bigr ) = \lim _{x \to a} f(x) - \lim _{x \to a} g(x) \]
Product Limit Law

\[ \lim _{x \to a} \bigl (f(x) \cdot g(x)\bigr ) = \bigl (\lim _{x \to a} f(x)\bigr ) \cdot \bigl (\lim _{x \to a} g(x) \bigr ) \]
Quotient Limit Law

\[ \lim _{x \to a} \left (\frac {f(x)}{g(x)}\right ) = \frac {\lim _{x \to a} f(x)}{\lim _{x \to a} g(x)},\ \text {provided} \lim _{x \to a} g(x) \ne 0 \]

Limit laws also hold for one-sided limits.
