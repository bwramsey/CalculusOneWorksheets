(b)
Suppose we are given the triangle below.
(i)
Find the length of the sides A and B.
This is an isosceles triangle. \(A=B\) Using the Pythagorean Theorem:
\begin{align*} A^2+B^2&=1^2\\ 2A^2&=1 \\ A^2&=\frac {1}{2}\\ A&=\sqrt {\frac {1}{2}}=\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\\ B&=\sqrt {\frac {1}{2}}=\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \end{align*}
(ii)
Express \(\sin \left (\frac {\pi }{4}\right )\) and \(\cos \left (\frac {\pi }{4}\right )\) in terms of the sides of the triangle.
\(\sin \left (\frac {\pi }{4}\right )=\frac {B}{C}=\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\) and \(\cos \left (\frac {\pi }{4}\right )=\frac {A}{C}=\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\)
