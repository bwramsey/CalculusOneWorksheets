Suppose that \( \displaystyle \int _{1}^{3} f(x)\d x=4 \)
(a)
Evaluate the following integrals.
(i)
\(\displaystyle \int _{0}^{2} f(x+1)\d x\)

(ii)
\(\displaystyle \int _{1}^{9} 3\dfrac {f(\sqrt {x})}{\sqrt {x}}\d x\)

(iii)
\(\displaystyle \int _{\frac {1}{3}}^{1} f(3x)\d x\)

(iv)
\(\displaystyle \int _{2}^{4} 3f(x-1)\d x\)

(v)
\(\displaystyle \int _{0}^{\sqrt {2}} 3x f(x^2+1)\d x\)

(b)
Assume that \(f\) is odd. Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int _{-3}^{-1} f(x)\d x\)

(c)
Assume that \(f\) is even. Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int _{-3}^{-1} f(x)\d x\)
