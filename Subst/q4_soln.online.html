Show that the areas of the three shaded regions in the figure below are equal. Find this area.
The area of the first shaded region is given by \(\int _{0}^{1} \sqrt {3x+1}\d x\).
We can evaluate this integral by substituting: \(u=3x+1\), \(\d u=3 \d x\); when \(x=0\), \(u=1\); when \(x=1\), \(u=4\).
Therefore \(\int _{0}^{1} \sqrt {3x+1}\d x= \int _{1}^{4} \dfrac {1}{3}\sqrt {u}\d u\)
and the last integral gives the area of the region in the middle.

The same integral can also be evaluated by substituting \(u=\sqrt {3x+1}\), \(\d u=\dfrac {3}{2\sqrt {3x+1}} \d x=\dfrac {3}{2u} \d x\);
when \(x=0\), \(u=1\); when \(x=1\), \(u=2\).
In this case we have

\(\int _{0}^{1} \sqrt {3x+1}\d x= \int _{1}^{2} u\cdot \dfrac {2}{3}u \d u=\int _{1}^{2} \dfrac {2}{3}u^2 \d u\).

The last integral gives the area of the third shaded region.
Therefore, the areas are equal.
