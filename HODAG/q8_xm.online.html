Suppose that a stone is thrown vertically upward from a cliff on Mars with an initial velocity of \(24\) ft/s from a height of \(192\) ft. The height \(s\) of the stone above the ground after \(t\) seconds is given by \(s(t) = -6t^2 + 24t + 192\).
(a)
Determine the velocity and acceleration of the stone after \(t\) seconds.
(b)
What is the greatest height of the stone and when does it occur? What are the velocity and acceleration at that time?
(c)
When does the stone hit the ground? What are the velocity and acceleration at that time?
