The total number of people, \(N\) who have contracted a common cold by a time \(t\) days after its outbreak on an island is given by \(N=N(t)= \dfrac {200000}{1+100e^{-0.1t}},t\geq 0\).
(a)
Evaluate and interpret the limit \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim _{t \to \infty } N(t)\)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{t \to \infty } N(t)=\displaystyle \lim _{t \to \infty } \dfrac {200000}{1+100e^{-0.1t}}=200000\). In the long run, 200000 people will get the cold.
(b)
How long will it take for the number of people who have contracted the cold to reach 40,000?
\begin{align*} N(t)&=40000\\ \dfrac {200000}{1+100e^{-0.1t}}&=40000\\ 200000&=40000(1+100e^{-0.1t})\\ 1+100e^{-0.1t}&=\dfrac {200000}{40000}\\ 100e^{-0.1t}&=5-1\\ e^{-0.1t}&=\dfrac {4}{100}\\ -0.1t&=\ln (.04)\\ t&=\dfrac {\ln (0.04)}{-0.1}=32.1888 \end{align*}

It will take 33 days for the number of people who have contracted the cold to reach 40,000.

(d)
Calculate \(N'(t)\). What does \(N'(t)\) represent?
\(N'(t)=\dfrac {-200000(10)e^{-0.1t}}{(1+100e^{-0.1t})^2}=\dfrac {2000000e^{-0.1t}}{(1+100e^{-0.1t})^2}\).
\(N'(t)\) represents the instantaneous growth rate of the number of people who have contracted the cold at the time \(t\).
(e)
Evaluate and interpret the limit \(\displaystyle \lim _{t \to \infty } N'(t)\)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{t \to \infty } N'(t)=\displaystyle \lim _{t \to \infty }\dfrac {2000000e^{-0.1t}}{(1+100e^{-0.1t})^2}=\dfrac {2000000(0)}{(1+100(0))^2}=\dfrac {0}{1}=0\).
In the long run, the number of people who have contracted the cold will stabilize. There will be no growth in the long run.
(f)
Find the average growth rate of the number of people who have contracted the disease during the time interval \([5,6]\) (or during the sixth day after the outbreak).
\(AVRG=\dfrac {N(6)-N(5)}{6-5}=N(6)-N(5)=\dfrac {200000}{1+100e^{-0.1(6)}}-\dfrac {200000}{1+100e^{-0.1(5)}} \approx 335.1\)
