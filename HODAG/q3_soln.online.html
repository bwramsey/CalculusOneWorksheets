Let \(f(x) = 2x^3-4x^2+1151\) and \(g(x) = x^3\sin (x)\). Compute \(f^{(4)}(x)\) and \(g^{(4)}(x)\).
Let’s find the first four derivatives of \(f\).
\begin{align*} f^{(0)}(x)&=2x^3-4x^2+1151\\ f^{(1)}(x)&=6x^2-8x\\ f^{(2)}(x)&=12x-8\\ f^{(3)}(x)&=12\\ f^{(4)}(x)&=0 \end{align*}

Let’s find the first four derivatives of \(g\).

\begin{align*} g^{(0)}(x)&=x^3\sin {x}\\ g^{(1)}(x)&=3x^2\sin {x}+x^3\cos {x}\\ g^{(2)}(x)&=6x\sin {x}+3x^2\cos {x}+3x^2\cos {x}-x^3\sin {x}\\ &=(6x-x^3)\sin {x}+(6x^2)\cos {x}\\ g^{(3)}(x)&=(6-3x^2)\sin {x}+(6x-x^3)\cos {x}+(12x)\cos {x}-(6x^2)\sin {x}\\ &=(6-3x^2-6x^2)\sin {x}+(6x-x^3+12x)\cos {x}\\ &=(6-9x^2)\sin {x}+(18x-x^3)\cos {x}\\ g^{(4)}(x)&=(-18x)\sin {x}+(6-9x^2)\cos {x}+(18-3x^2)\cos {x}-(18x-x^3)\sin {x}\\ &=(-18x-18x+x^3)\sin {x}+(6-9x^2+18-3x^2)\cos {x}\\ &=(-36x+x^3)\sin {x}+(24-12x^2)\cos {x} \end{align*}
