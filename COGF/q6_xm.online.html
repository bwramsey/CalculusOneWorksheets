Sketch the graph of a function \(f\) satisfying all of the conditions:

(a)
\(f\) is continuous and odd, \(f(0) = 0\),
(b)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x \to \infty } f(x) = -5\),
(c)
\(f'(x) > 0\) on \((6, \infty )\),
(d)
\(f'(x) < 0\) on \((0, 6)\),
(e)
\(f''(x) > 0\) on \((0, 12)\), and
(f)
\(f''(x) < 0\) on \((12, \infty )\).
