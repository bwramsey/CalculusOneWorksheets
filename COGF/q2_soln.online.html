Sketch a graph of a function that is continuous on \((-\infty ,\infty )\) that has the following properties.
(a)
Function \(f\) does not have a local maximum or minimum. \(f\) contains a point where \(f'(x)=0\)
(b)
\(g'(x)<0\) on \((-\infty , -1)\); \(g'(x)>0\) on \((-1,2)\); \(g'(x)<0\) on \((2,\infty )\).
