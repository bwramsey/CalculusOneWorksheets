Suppose an object is traveling along a straight line, with displacement given by a function \(s(t)\).

  • The average velocity, \(v_{av}\), of the object between time \(t=a\) and time \(t=b\) is given by

    \begin{align*} v_{av} &= \frac {\Delta s}{\Delta t} \\ &= \frac {s(b)-s(a)}{b-a} \end{align*}

  • The instantaneous velocity of the object at time \(t=a\) is given by

    \begin{align*} v(a) &= \lim _{\Delta t \to 0} \frac {\Delta s}{\Delta t}\\ &= \lim _{t\to a} \frac {s(t)-s(a)}{t-a}\\ &= \lim _{t \to a} v_{av}(t) \end{align*}

    where \(v_{av}(t)\) denotes the average velocity on the interval \([a,t]\) if \(t > a\), and \([t, a]\) if \(t < a\).
