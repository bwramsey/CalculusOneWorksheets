Given the following graphs of \(f\) and \(g\) (both piecewise linear functions), define new functions \(u(x) = f(g(x))\) and \(v(x) = f(x)g(x)\). Find:

(a)
\(u'(1)\)
\(u'(x) = \ddx (f(g(x))) = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)\). So,
\begin{align*} u'(1) &= f'(g(1)) \cdot g'(1) \\ &= f'(1) \cdot (-1) \\ &= (2)(-1) = -2 \end{align*}
(b)
\(v'(1)\)
\(v'(x) = \ddx (f(x)g(x)) = f'(x)g(x) + f(x)g'(x)\). So,
\begin{align*} v'(1) &= f'(1) g(1) + f(1) g'(1) \\ &= (2)(1) + (2)(-1) \\ &= 0 \end{align*}
(c)
\(\displaystyle \lim _{x\to 1} \dfrac {\sqrt {g(x)}-1}{x-1}\)
This is asking for \(\eval {\ddx ( \sqrt {g(x)} )}_{x=1}\). By the Chain Rule, \(\ddx ( \sqrt {g(x)} ) = \dfrac {g'(x)}{2\sqrt {g(x)}}\) so
\begin{align*} \eval {\ddx ( \sqrt {g(x)} )}_{x=1} &= \dfrac {g'(1)}{2\sqrt {g(1)}}\\ &= \dfrac {-1}{2\sqrt {1}}\\ &= -\dfrac {1}{2}. \end{align*}
