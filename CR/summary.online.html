• The Chain Rule: If \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) are differentiable functions, then \(\displaystyle \ddx f\left (g(x)\right ) = f'\left ( g(x)\right ) g'(x)\).

  • The Derivatives of Trigonometric Functions:

    \begin{align*} &\ddx \sin (x) = \cos (x) \quad & \ddx \cos (x) = -\sin (x) \\ &\ddx \tan (x) = \sec ^2(x) &\ddx \cot (x) = -\csc ^2(x) \\ &\ddx \sec (x) = \sec (x)\tan (x) &\ddx \csc (x) = -\csc (x)\cot (x) \end{align*}
