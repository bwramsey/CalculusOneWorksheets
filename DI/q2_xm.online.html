Let \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) be functions for which we only know the following:
\[ \int _1^4 f(x)\d x = 7 \qquad \int _2^4 f(x)\d x = 5 \qquad \int _1^4 g(x)\d x = 2 \]
Compute the following integrals, if possible. If it is not possible, give examples explaining why not.
(a)
\(\int _1^4 (8f(x) - 7g(x))\d x \)
(b)
\(\int _1^2 (-f(x)) \d x \)
(c)
\(\int _1^4 \left | f(x) \right | \d x\)
(d)
\(\int _1^4 \left ( 2 - x + f(x) \right ) \d x\)
