Evaluate the following sums:
(a)
\(\sum _{k=1}^{4} k^5 \)
\(\sum _{k=1}^{4}k^5 = 1^5 + 2^5 + 3^5 + 4^5 = 1 + 32 + 243 + 1024 = 1300\).
(b)
\(\sum _{k=1}^{400} (5(k+1)^2 + 3) \)
\begin{align*} \sum _{k=1}^{400} (5(k+1)^2 + 3) &= \sum _{k=1}^{400} (5(k^2 + 2k + 1) + 3) \\ &= \sum _{k=1}^{400} (5k^2 + 10k + 8) \\ &= \sum _{k=1}^{400} 5k^2 + \sum _{k=1}^{400} 10k + \sum _{k=1}^{400} 8 \\ &= 5\sum _{k=1}^{400}k^2 + 10 \sum _{k=1}^{400} k + 8(400) \\ &= 5 \left ( \frac {400(400+1)(2(400) + 1)}{6} \right ) + 10 \left ( \frac {400(400+1)}{2} \right ) + 3,200 \\ &= 5 (200)(401)(267) + 10(200)(401) + 3,200 \\ &= 107,067,000 + 802,000 + 3,200 = 107,872,200 \end{align*}
