Find the average value of the function \(g(t) = 4t e^{-t^2}\) on the interval \([0, 3]\).
We know \(\displaystyle g_{\text {avg}} = \frac {1}{b-a} \int _a^{b} g(t) \d t\) with \([a, b]\) as \([0,3]\). We’ll make a substitution, \(w = t^2\) with \(dw = 2t dt\). Notice that when \(t = 0\), \(w = (0)^2 = 0\) and when \(t=3\), \(w=(3)^2 = 9\).
\begin{align*} g_{\text {avg}} &= \frac {1}{3-0} \int _0^{3} 4 t e^{-t^2} \d t \\ &= \frac {1}{3} \int _0^9 2 e^{-w} \d w\\ &= \frac {1}{3} \eval {-2e^{-w}}_{0}^{9}\\ &= \frac {1}{3} \left [ (-2 e^{-9}) - (-2 e^{0}) \right ] \\ &= \frac {1}{3} \left ( 2 - \frac {2}{e^9} \right ) \end{align*}
