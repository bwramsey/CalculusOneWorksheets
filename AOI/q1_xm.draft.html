Solve the following word problems:

(a)
The velocity function for an object moving along a line east/west is given by \(v(t) = -t^2 + 4t - 3\) feet per minute.
(i)
Find the total displacement the object traveled from \(2\) minutes to \(6\) minutes (assume east is positive).

(ii)
Find the total distance the object traveled from \(2\) minutes to \(6\) minutes.

(iii)
Suppose that the object’s position \(2\) minutes into the trip is \(5\) feet of a placement marker. What is its position (relative to the placement marker) at \(6\) minutes.

(b)
Sammy the Snail sets up camp in the median of I-70 and, starting at noon and ending at 6pm, hikes back and forth along the highway. He starts his hike at his campsite. His velocity at time \(t\) hours (after noon) is given by \(v(t)=(t-2)(t-5)\) inches per hour. Find the total distance Sammy travelled on his hike.
