A cup of coffee has temperature \(20 + 75e^{-.02t}\) degrees (Celsius) \(t\) minutes after being poured into a cup. What is the average temperature of the coffee during the first half hour?
Let \(T(t) := 20 + 75e^{-.02t}\). We want the average value of \(T\) on \([0,30]\).
\begin{align*} T_{\text {avg}} &= \frac {1}{30-0} \int _0^{30} \left ( 20 + 75e^{-.02t} \right ) \d t \\ &= \frac {1}{30} \eval {20t - \frac {75}{.02} e^{-.02t}}_0^{30} \\ &= \frac {1}{30} \eval {20t - 3750 e^{-.02t}}_0^{30} \\ &= \frac {1}{30} \left [ \left ( 20(30) - 3750e^{-0.6} \right ) - (0 - 3750) \right ] \\ &= \frac {1}{30} \left ( 4350 - 3750e^{-0.6} \right ) \\ &= 145 - 125e^{-0.6} \end{align*}

So the average temperature of the coffee during the first half hour is \(145 - 125e^{-0.6} \approx 76.4\) degrees Celsius.
