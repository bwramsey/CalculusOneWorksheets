(a)
Let \(f(x) = \frac {x-1}{x^2 - 5x}\). Then \(f(2)=-\frac {1}{6}\) and \(f(6)=\frac {5}{6}\), but there is no value of \(c\) between \(2\) and \(6\) for which \(f(c)=0\). Does this fact violate the Intermediate Value Theorem?
It does not violate the Intermediate Value Theorem. \(f\) is not continuous at 5 so the conditions of the IVT do not hold and therefore the IVT does not apply.
(b)
True or False: At some time since you were born your weight in pounds exactly equaled your height in inches.
True: if \(w(t)\) represents your weight in pounds at time at time \(t\) and \(h(t)\) represents your height in inches at time \(t\), then \(w\) and \(h\) are both continuous functions. This implies \(w - h\) is also continuous. If \(t = 0\) is the moment you where born and \(t = T_0\) is the present time, then \(w(0) - h(0) < 0\) and \(w(T_0) - h(T_0) > 0\). Hence by the Intermediate Value Theorem there is a point in the past, \(t\), when \(w(t)-h(t)=0\) and therefore your weight in pounds equaled your height in inches.
