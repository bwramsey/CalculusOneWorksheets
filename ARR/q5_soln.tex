\begin{problem}
A conical water tank of height 10 feet and radius 4 feet is being drained through a hole in the bottom (See figure below).   
		\begin{image}
			\includegraphics[]{figure14.png}
		\end{image}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Let $r$ be the radius of the water at depth $h$.  In the figure above, label $r$ and $h$.\\
		\begin{explanation} 	
			\begin{image}
				\includegraphics[]{figure15.png}
			\end{image}
		\end{explanation}	
	\item Find an expression for $r$ in terms of $h$.
\begin{explanation}
		$\dfrac{r}{h}=\dfrac{R}{H}=\dfrac{4}{10}=\dfrac{2}{5} \implies r=\dfrac{2}{5}h$
	\end{explanation}
	
	\item The water in the tank is being drained at a rate of 2 feet$^3$ per minute.  What is the rate of change of the water depth when the water depth is 3 feet?
	\begin{explanation}
	Let $V$ be volume of water in the tank.  We were given $\dfrac{dV}{dt}=-2$ feet$^3$/min. \\
	We can relate the volume of water in the tank to the water height by the formula for the volume of a cone: $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$. \\\\
	We have already found $r$ in terms of $h$ so subsituting gives us:  $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi \left(\dfrac{2}{5}h\right)^2\cdot h=\dfrac{4}{75}\pi h^3$
	
	\begin{align*}
	V&=\dfrac{4}{75}\pi h^3\\
	\dfrac{dV}{dt}&=\dfrac{4}{75}\pi \cdot 3h^2 \cdot \dfrac{dh}{dt} \\
	\dfrac{dV}{dt}&=\dfrac{4}{25}\pi h^2 \cdot \dfrac{dh}{dt} \\
	-2&=\dfrac{4}{25}\pi (3)^2 \cdot \eval{\dfrac{dh}{dt}}_{h=3} \\
	\eval{\dfrac{dh}{dt}}_{h=3}&=-\dfrac{25}{18 \pi} ft/sec
	\end{align*}

\end{explanation}
\end{enumerate}
\end{problem}
