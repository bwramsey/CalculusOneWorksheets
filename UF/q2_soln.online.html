A mirror has the shape of a rectangle surmounted by a semicircle (see figure). The area of the mirror is \(32\) \(in^{2}\). Let \(x\) be the radius of the semicircle (that lies on top of the rectangle). Express the perimeter of the mirror P (in inches) as a function of \(x\) (in inches). Is P a polynomial, a rational function or a transcendental function?

Since the area \(A=32=2xy+\dfrac {x^{2}\pi }{2}\), it follows that \(y=\dfrac {16}{x}-\dfrac {x\pi }{4}\) and that \(P(x) = 2x + 2y +x\pi = 2x +\dfrac {32}{x}-\dfrac {x\pi }{2} +x\pi =2x+\dfrac {x\pi }{2}+\dfrac {32}{x}\)
