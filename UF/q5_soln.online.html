Define \(g(x) = \left \{ \begin {array}{cl} |x-2| & \qquad \text {if } x < 0 \\ \cos (x) & \qquad \text {if } x \geq 0 \\ \end {array} \right . \)
(a)
Sketch a graph of \(g\)

(b)
Find the domain and range of \(g\)
Domain: \((-\infty ,\infty )\), Range: \([-1,1]\cup (2,\infty )\)
(c)
Find the values of \(g(\pi )\) and \(g(-\pi )\)
\(g(\pi )=\cos (\pi )=-1\) and \(g(-\pi )=|-\pi -2|=\pi +2\)
