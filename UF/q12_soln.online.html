Explain what each of the following means:
(a)
\(f^{-1}(x)\)
This denotes the inverse function of \(f\), \(f^{-1}\), evauluated at \(x\).
(b)
\(f(x^{-1})\)
This means \(f \left ( \frac {1}{x} \right )\).
(c)
\(\left ( f(x) \right )^{-1}\)
This means \(f(x)\) raised to the \(-1\) power, i.e. \(\frac {1}{f(x)}\).
