Differentiate the function \(f\) defined by \(f(x) = 1/x^8\) in two different ways.
Applying the quotient rule:
\begin{align*} f'(x) &= \frac {(1)' \cdot x^8 - (1 \cdot (x^8)')}{(x^8)^2} \\ &= \frac {(0 \cdot x^8) - (1 \cdot 8x^7)}{x^{16}} \\ &= \frac {-8x^7}{x^{16}}\\ &= \frac {-8}{x^9}. \end{align*}

Applying the power rule:

\begin{align*} f(x) &= \frac {1}{x^8} = x^{-8}\\ &\implies f'(x) = (x^{-8})'\\ &= -8\cdot x^{-8 -1}\\ &= -8x^{-9} \end{align*}
