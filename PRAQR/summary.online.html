• The Product Rule: If \(f\) and \(g\) are differentiable functions, then \(\displaystyle \dfrac {d}{dx} \left ( f(x) g(x) \right ) = f'(x) g(x) + f(x)g'(x)\).
  • The Quotient Rule: If \(f\) and \(g\) are differentiable functions, then \(\displaystyle \dfrac {d}{dx} \dfrac { f(x)}{g(x)} = \dfrac {f'(x) g(x) - f(x)g'(x)}{g(x)^2}\).
