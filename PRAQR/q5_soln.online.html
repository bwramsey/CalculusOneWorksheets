Differentiate the following functions:
(a)
Given \(f(x) = (x^2 + 4x - 7) e^{-x}\), show \(f'(x)= (11-x^2 -2x) e^{-x}\).
\begin{align*} f'(x) &= \ddx \Bigl (\frac {x^2 + 4x - 7}{e^{x}}\Bigr ) \\ &= \frac {(2x+4)e^{x}-(x^2 + 4x - 7)e^{x}}{e^{2x}} \\ &= \frac {e^{x}(2x + 4-x^2 - 4x + 7)}{e^{2x}} \\ &= \frac {11-x^2 - 2x }{e^{x}} \\ &=(11-x^2 -2x) e^{-x} \end{align*}
(b)
Given \(g(x) = \frac {x^2 + 4x - 7}{e^{-x}}\), show \(g'(x)=\frac {x^2 + 6x - 3}{e^{-x}}\)
\begin{align*} g'(x) &= \ddx \Bigl (\frac {x^2 + 4x - 7}{e^{-x}}\Bigr ) \\ &= \ddx \Bigl ((x^2 + 4x - 7)e^{x} \Bigr ) \\ &= \ddx (x^2 + 4x - 7)e^{x} +(x^2 + 4x - 7)\ddx e^{x}\\ &=(2x+4)e^{x}+(x^2 + 4x - 7)e^{x}\\ &=e^{x}(2x+4+x^2 + 4x - 7)\\ &=e^{x}(x^2 + 6x - 3)\\ &=\frac {x^2 + 6x - 3}{e^{-x}}\\ \end{align*}
