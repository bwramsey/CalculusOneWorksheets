Differentiate the functions using product or quotient rule.
(a)
\(h(u)=7ue^u\)
Applying the power rule:
\begin{align*} h(u) &=7ue^u\\ &\implies h'(u) = (7ue^u) '\\ &= 7\cdot e^u+7u \cdot e^u \end{align*}
(b)
\(s(t)=\frac {\sqrt {t}}{e^t}\)
Applying the quotient rule:
\begin{align*} s(t)&=\frac {\sqrt {t}}{e^t}\\ &\implies s'(t) = \left (\frac {\sqrt {t}}{e^t}\right )' \\ &= \frac {\frac {1}{2}t^{-1/2} \cdot e^t - \sqrt {t}\cdot e^t}{e^{2t}} \end{align*}
