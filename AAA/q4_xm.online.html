The velocity function for a man walking along a straight road which runs east and west is given by \(v(t) = -t^2 + 4t - 3\) ft/min.
(a)
Set up a definite integral for the man’s displacement during the time interval from \(2\) minutes to \(6\) minutes after he began running.
(b)
At home: Evaluate the definite integral using the limit of a right Riemann sum.
(c)
Is this the same as the total distance the man walked from \(2\) minutes to \(6\) minutes? Why or why not?
