(a)
Write as an exponential with base \(5\). \(\displaystyle 7^{3x}\).
\(7 = 5^{\log _5(7)}\), so
\[ 7^{3x} = \left ( 5^{\log _5(7)} \right )^{3x} = 5^{3x \log _5(7)} \]
(b)
Write in terms of the natural logarithm. \(\displaystyle \log _3\left ( 4\right )\).
The change of base formula says \(\displaystyle \log _a(u) = \frac {\log _b(u)}{\log _b(a)}\), so
\[ \log _3(4) = \frac {\ln (4)}{\ln (3)} \]
(c)
Expand the following: \(\displaystyle \log _{1/2} \left ( \frac {6x^5(2+\tan (x))^x}{\sqrt [5]{e^{4x}+1}} \right )\).
