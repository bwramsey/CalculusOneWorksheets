Useful formulas:   (1) \(\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}C =n\cdot C\) ;   (2) \(\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n} k =\dfrac {n(n+1)}{2}\);    (3) \(\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n} k^2 =\dfrac {n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}\)    (3) \(\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n} k^3 =\dfrac {n^2(n+1)^2}{4}\)
Useful example of application of rules for sums:
\(\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}(ak+b) =\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}ak + \displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}b=a\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}k + \displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}b=a\dfrac {n(n+1)}{2}+nb\), where a and b constants.

SUMMARY of Riemann Sums:

Riemann sum: \(\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{n}f(x_k^*)\Delta x\),
Right Riemann sum: \(x_k^*=x_k\); Left Riemann sum: \(x_k^*=x_{k-1}\); Midpoint Riemann sum: \(x_k^*=\dfrac {x_{k-1}+x_k}{2}\).
Width of each of \(n\) rectangles on the interval \([a,b]\): \(\Delta x=\dfrac {b-a}{n}\)
