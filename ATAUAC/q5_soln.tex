\begin{problem}
The graph of the function $f(x)=x^2$ is given in the figure.

   
      \begin{image}
      \includegraphics[]{figurer001.png}
      \end{image}
    Find the exact value of the area of the shaded region. HINT: Use a right Riemann sum with $n$ rectangles, and  then take the limit as $n\to\infty$.
      \begin{explanation}
 $\Delta x=\dfrac{2}{n}$\\
  $ x_k^*=x_k=k\Delta x=k\left(\dfrac{2}{n}\right)=\dfrac{2k}{n}$, for $k=1,...,n$\\

 $A\approx \displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}  f ( x_k^* ) \Delta x = \Delta x\cdot\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}  f ( x_k^* )=\dfrac{2}{n}\cdot\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n} f \left( \dfrac{2k}{n} \right)=\dfrac{2}{n}\cdot\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}  \left( \dfrac{2k}{n} \right)^2= \dfrac{2}{n}\cdot\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}  \dfrac{4k^2}{n^2} = \dfrac{8}{n^3}\cdot\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}k^2 $ \\[1em]
Recall: $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n} k^2 =\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$\\
Therefore\\
 $A\approx\dfrac{8}{n^3}\cdot\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}k^2 =\dfrac{8}{n^3}\cdot\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} =\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{n^2}=\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2n^2+3n+1}{n^2}$.\\[1em]
 Let's take the limit of Riemann sums as $n\to\infty$.\\[1em]
  $A=\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty} \displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n}  f ( x_k^* ) \Delta x =\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2n^2+3n+1}{n^2}=\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\ \left(2+\dfrac{3}{n}+\dfrac{1}{n^2}\right)=\dfrac{8}{3}$.

\end{explanation}
\end{problem}
