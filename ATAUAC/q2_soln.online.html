(a)
If a function is positive and decreasing on an interval \([a,b]\), will a right Riemann sum underestimate or overestimate the area of the region under the graph of the function? Justify your answer.
Because the function is decreasing, a right Riemann sum will be an underestimate. By definition, \(f(x_k)< f(x)\) for all \(x\) in the interval \([x_{k-1},x_k]\) .
(b)
If a function is positive and decreasing on an interval \([a,b]\), will a left Riemann sum underestimate or overestimate the area of the region under the graph of the function? Justify your answer.
Because the function is decreasing, a right Rieimann sum will be an overestimate. By definition, \(f(x_{k-1})> f(x)\) for all \(x\) in the interval \([x_{k-1},x_k]\) .
