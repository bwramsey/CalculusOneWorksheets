For each of the following functions, use the "short cut derivative rules" to compute their derivative.
(a)
\(f(x) = \sqrt {x}\)
\[f(x) = \sqrt {x} = x^{\frac {1}{2}}.\]
\begin{align*} f'(x) &= \frac {1}{2} x^{\frac {1}{2} - 1}\\ &= \frac {1}{2} x^{-\frac {1}{2}} \\ &= \frac {1}{2\sqrt {x}} \end{align*}
(b)
\(s(u) = \frac {5}{u^2}\)
\[s(u) = \frac {5}{u^2} = 5u^{-2}.\]
\begin{align*} s'(u) &= 5(-2) u^{-2-1}\\ &= -10u^{-3}\\ &= \frac {-10}{u^3} \end{align*}
(c)
\(p(t) = t^5 + 4t^3 + \pi \)
\begin{align*} p'(t) &= 5t^{5-1} + 4(3)t^{3-1} + 0\\ &= 5t^4 + 12t^2 \end{align*}

Note that \(\ddx (\pi ) = 0\) because \(\pi \) is a constant.
