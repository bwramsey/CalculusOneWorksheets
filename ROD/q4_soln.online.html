(c)
Compute the value of the \((5f+3g)'(2)\).
\begin{align*} (5f+3g)'(2) &= 5\cdot f'(2) + 3 \cdot g'(2)\\ &= 5 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 1 \\ &= 20 + 3 = 23. \end{align*}
(d)
Find the equation of the tangent line to the graph of \((5f+3g)\) at the point where \(x=2\).
\begin{align*} (5f+3g)(2) &= 5\cdot f(2) + 3 \cdot g(2)\\ &= 5 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 2 \\ &= 20 + 6 = 26\\\\ y-26 &=23(x-2)\\ y&=23x-20 \end{align*}
